OVERVIEW

Build What Truly Matters

Bring your team of four to six and solve a problem that matters to people. You submit your idea first, then build a prototype during the 24-hour hackathon.

There is a single round. Every update, instruction and announcement goes to your Team Leader's mobile number and email, so make sure both are correct when you register.

Leader Contact Precision Notice

All official team credentials, access keys, track guidelines, and hackathon schedules will be dispatched directly to the Team Leader's mobile number and email ID. Please ensure 100% accuracy during submission.

CHALLENGE DOMAINS

Three Pillars of Innovation

Select one thematic track for your team's proposal. Build practical hardware or software solutions that make a measurable impact.

TRACK 01

Human Safety, Rights & Empowerment

Solutions aimed at vulnerable communities, digital security, emergency response networks, accessibility for disabled citizens, and women's safety.

TRACK 02

Health, Education & Digital Innovation

Accessible rural healthcare diagnostics, assistive learning engines, Indic language tutoring, telemedicine, and localized digital empowerment tools.

TRACK 03

Sustainability, Smart Communities & Social Entrepreneurship

Clean renewable microgrids, waste lifecycle tracking, sustainable agriculture IoT, decentralized circular economies, and urban resilience systems.
